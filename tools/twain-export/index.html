What’s in the download?

Your original Move .ablbundle, scene-length Twain WAVs, separate FX tails, and the original Twain save. Muted clips are included. Open the Move Set in Live, then add the Twain WAVs to audio tracks; this prototype does not automatically arrange them in Live.

FX tails are kept separately so loops stay in time. They are limited to 20 seconds and fade at the end. Export starts each clip’s effects from cleared tail memory rather than capturing a live performance.

Use files already on this laptop
